About this event

Spring Hackathon 2025 is a month-long hackathon open to teams of up to 6 members. Projects can fall under any of our 3 tracks: Web & Mobile, AI & Machine Learning, and Open Innovation.

Submissions will be reviewed by a panel of 12 expert judges. Winners will be announced during the live results ceremony on May 5th.

Tracks

🌐

Web & Mobile

Build production-ready web or mobile applications. Any framework, any stack.

🤖

AI & Machine Learning

Leverage models, fine-tuning, or custom training to solve real problems.

💡

Open Innovation

Any category. Any problem. The most creative submission wins.

Timeline

Apr 1 — Registration Opens

Teams can register and form. Invite codes generated on team creation.

Apr 5 — Submissions Open

Teams can begin submitting draft projects. Editable until deadline.

Apr 30 — Submission Deadline

All submissions must be finalised. No edits after this date. (Invariant I11)

May 5 — Results Published

Winners announced. Certificates generated and emailed.
